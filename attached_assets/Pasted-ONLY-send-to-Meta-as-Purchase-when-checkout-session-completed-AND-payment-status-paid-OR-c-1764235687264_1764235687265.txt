ONLY send to Meta as "Purchase" when:

checkout.session.completed AND payment_status === "paid"
OR checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded

NEVER send to Meta when:

payment_intent.payment_failed
checkout.session.async_payment_failed
checkout.session.completed with payment_status === "unpaid" or "no_payment_required"
