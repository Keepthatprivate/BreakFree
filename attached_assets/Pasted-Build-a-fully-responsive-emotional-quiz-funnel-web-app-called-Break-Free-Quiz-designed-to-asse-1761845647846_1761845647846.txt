Build a fully responsive emotional quiz funnel web app called **“Break Free Quiz”** designed to assess childhood trauma patterns and convert visitors into leads for a healing app. The quiz should feel immersive, personal, and varied — avoiding repetitive layouts. Use **HTML, TailwindCSS, and JavaScript (or React if simpler)**.

---

### 🎯 GOAL
Create a **multi-format, emotionally engaging experience** where each question or break screen feels alive, human, and different — combining empathy, psychology, and visual storytelling.  
Users should feel guided through a reflective journey, not like they’re filling out a form.

---

### 💎 CORE FLOW (4 Chapters)
1. **Your Past** — nostalgic tone, warm light (beige/sepia), slow fades.  
2. **Your Patterns** — introspective tone, cooler tones (light blue/grey), slide-in questions.  
3. **Your Healing** — hopeful tone, brighter gradients, growth visuals.  
4. **Your Future** — empowering tone, white/blue palette, clean modern design.

Show a **progress bar with 4 dots** at the top (each labeled by chapter).

---

### 🧭 QUESTION FORMATS (ROTATE BETWEEN)
1. **Multiple-choice cards** (rounded, soft hover animation)  
2. **Quote reflection format** →  
   “I often hide pain behind humor.”  
   → 4 emotional reaction buttons (Strongly relate / Somewhat / Hardly / Not at all)
3. **Popup question** → fade-in modal, blurred background (“Do you often feel on edge?”)  
4. **Slider scale** → For intensity-based questions (“How much time are you willing to dedicate…”)  
5. **Emoji grid** → For emotion-based questions (“What do you feel when seeing loved children?”)  
6. **Image-choice cards** → Visual answers (e.g. peaceful room, chaotic home)  
7. **Info / Data / Quote Breaks** → See below 👇  

Each question or break should appear with a subtle motion (fade, slide-up, or pop-in) and a **0.3–0.5s delay** to create rhythm and anticipation.  
Inject **empathy microcopy** randomly between steps (“You’re doing great.” “Awareness is healing.” “This reflection takes courage.”).

---

### 📊 INFO / DATA / QUOTE BREAKS (NEW)
Insert 1–2 per chapter to reduce fatigue and increase authority & emotional connection.

Each break can take one of these **formats**:

**1. Data Card**
> “🧠 Harvard School of Public Health found that people who experienced childhood neglect have a 30% higher risk of chronic stress.”  
→ Subtext: “But awareness and guided healing can reverse much of that impact.”

**2. Quote Reflection**
> “It’s never too late to have a happy childhood.”  
> — Tom Robbins  
→ Subtext: “Many people begin healing in adulthood — and that’s powerful.”

**3. Visual Pause**
→ Fullscreen or half-screen photo/illustration (e.g. a sunrise, a hand letting go of rope)  
→ Overlay short text: “Every question brings you closer to understanding.”

**4. Testimonial Break**
> “Break Free helped me stop repeating painful patterns. I finally feel peace.”  
> — *James, 34* ⭐ 4.9 rating

**Behavior:**
- Auto-fade-in, small breathing delay (2–3 seconds before “Continue” button appears).  
- Blend empathy + insight: users feel *seen* and *educated*.  
- Optional subtle animation (slow parallax image movement).

---

### 🧠 INTERACTION LOGIC
- Randomly select animation styles per question to keep flow dynamic.  
- Allow previous navigation with smooth back animation.  
- Auto-save answers in localStorage.  
- Begin each chapter with a short title animation (“Chapter 2: Your Patterns”).

---

### 🎨 VISUAL STYLE
- **Typography:** “Nunito” or “Inter” (rounded, warm feel).  
- **Color Palette:** Beige, soft green, muted blue (#2563eb → #003666).  
- **Design Language:** Rounded corners, glassy cards, soft shadows, airy spacing.  
- **Backgrounds:**  
  - Faint gradient overlays or blurred imagery.  
  - Occasional animated particles or line art for depth.  
- **Buttons:** Green primary (“Next”), rounded, glow hover, gentle tap feedback.
